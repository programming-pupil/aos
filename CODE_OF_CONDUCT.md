# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want AOS to be a welcoming, professional, and practical open-source
community. Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to communicate with
respect and assume good intent while still being clear about technical risks.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, code, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find the right docs or issues.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.
- Keep security-sensitive details out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Spam, impersonation, or malicious reports.
- Pressuring maintainers to disclose private security details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or ban
accounts that violate this code. Serious security or abuse reports should be sent
privately to maintainers.
